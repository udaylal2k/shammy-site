Your trainer

Taught by someone who still works the floor.

Roopa G.R. is a hair, skin and makeup trainer with international certification and 22 years of experience. She began as a trainer for L’Oréal’s MATRIX division in Chennai, ran salon workshops and product launches, and has led her own salon in Davangere since 2015.

Her own training includes the L’Oréal Hair Academy, TONI&GUY Singapore, Pivot Point, Kryolan and Thalion, France. Students learn the way she works: consult first, choose the right product, keep the workstation clean and deliver a consistent result.

  • L’Oréal Hair Academy
  • TONI&GUY Singapore
  • Pivot Point & Kryolan
  • Thalion, France

Three months. Three crafts.

One programme, taught in order, so every student leaves with hair, skin and makeup skills rather than just one.

  1. 01

    Hair

    • Hair and scalp science (trichology)
    • Choosing professional products
    • Cutting techniques
    • Colour theory and colouring
    • Chemical services: keratin, smoothening and straightening
  2. 02

    Skin

    • Skin anatomy and skin types
    • Operating facial machines
    • Professional facial treatments, including single-use facial kits
    • Hygiene and consultation before every treatment
  3. 03

    Makeup

    • Skin preparation and a clean base
    • Professional makeup techniques
    • Working with professional kits
    • Bridal and occasion looks

Live internship

Then you work in a real salon.

The programme ends with a practical rotation inside a busy salon. Students sit in on client consultations, learn the reception desk and the flow of the day, and practise keeping a workstation clean and ready.

Graduates receive a Shaamy Lounge Academy certificate.

What you leave with.

Technique

Hands-on skill in hair, skin and makeup, practised on real clients.

Product knowledge

How to choose and use professional products for each hair and skin need.

Client handling

How to consult, listen and recommend, so every service fits the person.

Confidence

The discipline and professionalism to work independently, or start something of your own.
