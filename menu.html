Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to book in advance?
Yes, we recommend booking at least 1 week ahead for regular services and 1 month ahead for bridal packages. Walk-ins are welcome subject to availability.
What products do you use?
We exclusively use L'Oréal Professional for color & care, Kerastase for treatments, and clinical-grade actives for skin.
Do you offer bridal trials?
Yes! Every bridal package includes a complimentary trial session (2 hrs, 2 looks). Standalone trials are ₹2,500 and credited toward your package.
What is your cancellation policy?
24-hour notice for regular services. Bridal packages require 72-hour notice. No-shows are charged 50% of service value.
Do you do group bookings for bridal parties?
Yes! We offer group discounts for 3+ bridal party members. Book together and save 10% per person.
