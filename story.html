About the salon

One of Davangere’s first unisex salons.

Founded in 2015, Shaamy Lounge stands proud as one of the first unisex salons in Davangere: a place where grooming, self-care and artistry come together in a warm and welcoming environment. For over a decade the salon has earned the trust of its clients through consistent quality, professionalism and a deep-rooted passion for the craft.

At the heart of this space is Roopa G.R, a highly accomplished hair stylist, makeup artist, colorist and skin consultant with more than 22 years of experience in the beauty industry, and a hair, skin and makeup trainer with international certification. She is admired for her clarity, creativity, punctuality, sincerity and honesty.

In Roopa’s words

A journey of passion, courage and learning.

  1. The beginning

    A trainer at L’Oréal

    My career began with an opportunity to work as a professional trainer for the MATRIX division of L’Oréal in Chennai. This experience gave me a strong foundation in technical education, professional hairdressing and the science behind hair services.

    Working with salon professionals, conducting workshops, participating in product launches and understanding the evolving requirements of the beauty industry helped me develop a broader vision of what professional beauty services should represent.

  2. Growing beyond the salon floor

    Understanding the whole industry

    During my association with L’Oréal’s MATRIX division, I gained experience in technical training, salon education, product launches, market visits, product development support, research and development coordination, technical analysis and team management.

  3. 2010 to 2013

    Managing Partner, WOW Boutique Salon & Spa

    This role gave me valuable exposure to salon operations, professional team grooming, client consultation, product management and business development. These experiences shaped my understanding of the beauty industry from both technical and entrepreneurial perspectives.

  4. 2015

    Turning a dream into reality

    In 2015, I took an important step in my entrepreneurial journey by establishing Shaamy Lounge Unisex Saloon & Spa in Davangere. My vision was to create a professional beauty destination where clients could experience personalised services, quality products, hygiene and professional standards under one roof.

    At Shaamy Lounge, we focus on Hair, Skin and Makeup services for men, women and children.

  5. Today

    Still growing

    Today Shaamy Lounge continues to flourish, a reflection of Roopa’s commitment to detail, learning and service. Every element of the salon, from its aesthetic to its team, is guided by her belief that beauty is not just about how you look, but how you feel. And it’s not temporary, it’s everlasting.

My philosophy

Never stop learning.

The beauty industry is constantly evolving. New techniques, technologies, products and professional standards emerge every day. I believe that a true beauty professional must remain a student throughout their career.

Experience gives us confidence, but continuous learning keeps us relevant.

I believe that every individual has different needs. Understanding hair texture, scalp condition, skin type and personal expectations is essential before recommending any treatment. My approach has always been to customise services rather than follow a routine or one-size-fits-all method.

My vision

Empowering the next generation.

One of the most meaningful parts of my journey is the opportunity to share my knowledge with others. Through Shaamy Lounge Academy, I aspire to help young women and men discover their potential in the beauty industry.

I want students to understand that beauty is not merely a short-term skill or a certificate. It can become a rewarding profession, a source of financial independence and an opportunity to build an entrepreneurial future.

I want every student who learns with me to leave with more than technical knowledge: confidence, discipline and the courage to create their own identity.

A message to every dreamer

Your background does not define your future.

Success does not happen overnight. There will be challenges, unexpected changes, difficult decisions and moments when you question yourself. But never allow temporary difficulties to become permanent limitations.

Invest in your education. Respect your profession. Be willing to learn from everyone. Believe in your abilities, and never be afraid to start again or start something of your own.

I am still learning, evolving and dreaming bigger. My journey is not just about building a salon. It is about building confidence, creating opportunities, sharing knowledge and inspiring others to believe in themselves.
