Our Story

Sustained by excellence. The story of Shaamy Lounge and the woman behind it.

Founded in 2015, Shaamy Lounge stands proud as one of the first unisex salons in Davangere: a place where grooming, self-care and artistry come together in a warm and welcoming environment. For nearly a decade the salon has earned the trust of its clients through consistent quality, professionalism and a deep-rooted passion for the craft.

At the heart of this space is Roopa G.R, a highly accomplished hair stylist, makeup artist, colorist and skin consultant with over 20 years of experience in the beauty industry. Her journey began in Chennai, where she started as a trainer for L’Oréal’s MATRIX division. Even at the start of her career she stood out for her initiative and leadership, leading workshops, launch events, technical trainings, salon management programmes and product education.

Over the years Roopa has built an exceptional portfolio of qualifications. She holds a diploma from the L’Oréal Hair Academy, has completed both Creative Coloring and Classic Cutting courses with TONI&GUY in Singapore, and received foundational to advanced training in makeup from Pivot Point and Kryolan. She further honed her skills under Dominic Cruz, the internationally recognised Miss Universe makeup artist, and completed advanced skincare training with Thalion in France. With a foundation in Nutrition and Dietetics and a Level 1 certification from City & Guilds, her approach to beauty is both holistic and highly professional.

Roopa is admired for her clarity, creativity, punctuality, sincerity and honesty. Whether it is a bridal makeover, a self-grooming workshop or a casual haircut, her work is deeply personal and consistently professional.

Today Shaamy Lounge continues to flourish, a reflection of Roopa’s commitment to detail, learning and service. Every element of the salon, from its aesthetic to its team, is guided by her belief that beauty is not just about how you look, but how you feel. And it’s not temporary, it’s everlasting.
