Why Shaamy Lounge.

Shaamy Lounge opened in 2015 as one of the first unisex salons in Davangere, a warm and welcoming place where grooming, self-care and artistry come together.

The men’s menu covers cuts, shaving, beard and moustache colouring, L’Oréal straightening and keratin, dandruff treatment, facials, de-tan, trimming and waxing.
