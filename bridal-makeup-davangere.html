Bridal questions, answered.

Do you offer a bridal makeup trial in Davangere?

Yes. You can book a trial MAC makeup and a trial hair do before the wedding to settle the look. Message Shaamy Lounge on WhatsApp for available dates.

Which makeup options do you offer for brides?

Complete Kryolan, MAC, HD and airbrush makeup, along with party and advanced party makeup, saree draping and Toppik hair filler.

Do you do bridesmaid makeup?

Yes. Bridesmaid makeup is available as regular, Kryolan, MAC, and MAC with concealing and contouring.

Which facials can I have before the wedding?

The bridal facials are the Save the Date Bridal Facial, the O3 Bridal Facial and the Janssen Bridal Whitening Facial.

How much does bridal makeup cost?

Prices are shared when you book. Message us on WhatsApp with the look you want, and we will reply with times and prices.

Who will do my makeup?

Roopa G.R., the founder of Shaamy Lounge, who trained with Pivot Point and Kryolan and honed her skills under Dominic Cruz, the internationally recognised Miss Universe makeup artist.
