Our Story

Shaamys Lounge was born from a simple belief: beauty is confidence, care, and craft. Not a luxury for the few, but a ritual for everyone who walks through our doors.

Founded by Roopa G.R., an award-winning hair, skin & bridal artist with over 20 years of experience. Her journey spans continents and the world's most respected academies:

  • TONI&GUY Singapore — Advanced cutting & coloring techniques
  • Pivot Point Academy — Structural hair design & precision
  • L'Oréal Professional Academy — Color mastery & skin science

Today, Shaamys Lounge stands as Davangere's leading luxury unisex salon — a space where international standards meet personal attention. Every service, from a simple trim to a full bridal transformation, is delivered with the same precision, hygiene, and heart.
